Careers

Build with purpose.

We value curiosity, ownership and clear thinking — engineers who are comfortable asking why before how.

How we think about the work

Engineers who start with the problem.

AU4’s work sits where business context, product thinking, engineering, cloud, data and security meet. Nobody is expected to be an expert in all of it — but the best people are curious enough to see how a decision in their area ripples through the rest of the system and the business.

We value specialists who can still see the system around their speciality.

Deep in one area. Curious about the rest.

Bring real depth somewhere, understand the concerns next door, and collaborate across the boundaries rather than defending them.

What we value in people

Six things we look for.

Less about a specific stack, more about how someone approaches the work.

Curiosity before assumption

Ask why before deciding how.

Ownership of outcomes

Care whether the work actually improves something — not just whether a ticket closes.

Clear communication

Explain technical choices so other people can make informed decisions.

Engineering judgment

Understand trade-offs rather than defaulting to the newest or most complex technology.

Security & maintainability

Treat both as part of normal engineering quality, not as extras.

“We shouldn’t build this”

Sometimes the strongest engineering decision is not introducing another system.

The company we’re building

How we want AU4 to feel to work in.

These are operating principles for the company AU4 is intentionally building — not a claim that a mature culture already exists.

Small teams

Short communication paths, less lost between people.

Visible context

Everyone should understand why the work matters.

Room to challenge

Technical decisions should stay discussable, not handed down.

Document what matters

Decisions should survive beyond the meeting they were made in.

Learn across disciplines

Cloud, product, data and security shouldn’t become isolated silos.

Quality without theatre

Enough process to make work reliable — not process for its own sake.

Current status

We’re not actively hiring right now.

AU4 is being built deliberately. When roles open, we’ll publish them here — each with its responsibilities, expectations, working model and a clear way to apply.

Until then there’s nothing to apply to, and this site collects no candidate details. Check back here when roles open.
