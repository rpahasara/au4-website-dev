WHAT CHANGED IN THIS VERSION
  - Navigation preview dialogs replaced with real pages and relative links.
  - Refined violet primary CTA: compact pill, smooth gradient, brighter upper surface, darker lower
    edge, inner highlight, restrained shadow, gentle hover and pressed states. Applied as a reusable
    .button.violet style; white, dark, outline and text-link variants are unchanged.
  - Shared styles.css and script.js across all pages. Sub-pages reuse the approved dark hero language,
    pale content sections, cards, spacing and motion.
  - Dialogs retained only for small interactions: site search (now linking to real pages), the story
    storyboard and the client-content note.
  - Homepage design, section order, imagery, density and motion are unchanged.
