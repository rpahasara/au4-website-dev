What happens next

  1. 01

    We read the context

    At AU4’s current stage, a founder reviews the inquiry and the problem you’ve described.

  2. 02

    We ask questions

    If it looks relevant, we follow up to understand the current situation, the outcome you want, the constraints that matter and the people affected — not to send a proposal.

  3. 03

    We have a short conversation

    A short call to work out whether there’s a useful problem we can responsibly help with — before any technology is recommended.

  4. 04

    We define a first move

    If there’s a good fit, we define the smallest useful next step — a focused assessment, an architecture or workflow review, technical discovery, a prototype, or a clearly defined delivery increment.

At AU4’s current stage, new inquiries are reviewed directly by the founding team. There’s no obligation in reaching out — a first conversation is simply about understanding the problem.

Before you write

You don’t need the solution yet.

You don’t have to arrive with a technical answer. Plenty of good engagements begin with a clear description of a problem and nothing more — the right technology, if any, is something we work out together, once the context is understood.

Bring the problem. We’ll help shape the response.

You don’t need

  • A finished requirements document
  • A chosen cloud provider
  • A defined AI use case
  • A final architecture
  • A feature backlog
  • Perfect technical terminology

What actually helps

  • What is happening now
  • Where the friction shows up
  • Who it affects
  • What “better” would look like
  • Any constraints you already know

How we engage

Three engagement shapes — not packages.

These describe how work with AU4 tends to take shape. They are not pricing tiers, and you don’t need to move through all three.

01

Focused assessment

A small initial engagement, used when a problem deserves proper investigation before any delivery is committed.

  • Might produce
  • A current-state and workflow review
  • An architecture view
  • Prioritized, risk-informed recommendations
  • A clearly defined first move
02

Defined delivery

A clearly scoped outcome we design and deliver through small, reviewable increments — best when the first useful scope, ownership and constraints are clear.

  • You get
  • Visible progress, not a black box
  • Reviewable increments
  • Clear trade-offs along the way
  • Quality and security built into delivery
03

Ongoing partnership

We work alongside your team as a product, platform or system keeps evolving, staying close to the decisions.

  • Might cover
  • Product evolution
  • Platform & cloud improvement
  • Delivery & DevOps
  • Security and data/AI workflows

Most engagements start small. Focused assessment → defined delivery → ongoing partnership is a common path, not a requirement — many begin directly with defined delivery, and some only ever need an assessment.

Honest fit

If we’re not the right partner, we’ll say so.

We’d rather be honest about fit than sell the wrong engagement. There are situations where AU4 is not the best answer, and we’ll tell you when we see one:

  • The need is mainly for specialist legal, regulatory or compliance advice — expertise we work alongside, not something we provide ourselves.
  • The request is outside what we can responsibly deliver today. We’d rather say so than stretch to fit.
  • The real need is extra engineering hands to a fixed specification, rather than shared responsibility for solving a problem. That’s a valid model — just a different one from how we work.
  • There isn’t yet enough context for us to recommend the requested solution responsibly. In that case we’ll say what we’d want to understand first.
