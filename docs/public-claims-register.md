# AU4 Labs Public Claims Register

Internal guardrail for website and content work. **Not a public page — do not deploy inside `au4-site/`.**
Purpose: keep every public-facing claim honest and defensible for an early-stage company.

_Last updated: 2026-09-21 (Phase 12 — global consistency polish)._

---

## Approved factual claims
Statements currently safe to publish because they are true today.

- AU4 Labs is an engineering-led technology/product company.
- AU4 Labs works across four disciplines: Product Engineering; Cloud & DevOps; Data & AI; Cyber Security.
- Positioning line: **"Turn Friction Into Advantage."**
- The founding team has hands-on professional experience across software/web engineering, cloud, DevOps/SRE, cybersecurity, corporate IT, infrastructure and data/AI-related engineering.
- AU4 Labs has **four founders**, whose collective experience spans the areas above. No individual founder names, biographies, photos, employers, titles or years of experience are published (none are verified/available yet).
- AU4 Labs is **based in Sri Lanka** and is being built for distributed, cross-border collaboration. This states location and intent only — **not** existing international offices or international clients.
- At AU4's **current stage**, discovery and delivery are **founder-led**. Framed as the present operating model, never as a permanent guarantee.
- The website sets no cookies, uses no analytics/tracking, and has no backend; the contact form runs in the browser only and transmits/stores nothing.

## Capability claims
What AU4 can do — framed as capability and intended outcome, **not** as historical customer evidence.

- Design, build, secure and scale digital products and platforms.
- Modernize legacy platforms incrementally; connect disconnected tools; improve operational efficiency; launch digital products; strengthen resilience and security.
- Security considered at architecture/design time.
- Delivery in small, reviewable increments with tests, documentation and clean handover.
- Outcomes framed as targets: "outcomes we work toward" — e.g. faster/calmer releases, lower operating friction, clearer decisions, reduced risk. **Never** as achieved results with numbers.
- "Engineering experience across modern technology ecosystems": AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, Kubernetes, Docker, GitHub, GitLab — presented as **experience**, never as partnerships, certifications or customers.

## Aspirational / positioning language
Brand language safe to use because it asserts a stance, not a track record.

- "More than a tech company. A long-term partner."
- "Business outcome first. Technology second."
- "Built for global teams" / "Built for international collaboration" — means designed to serve international clients, **not** that global clients already exist.
- "Engineering a smarter tomorrow."
- Values/principles: Innovation, Security, Partnership, Impact; Expert Team, Disciplined Delivery, Global Perspective, Client-Centric.
- Honest positioning: "We do not claim sector credentials we have not earned."

## Claims requiring evidence
**Must NOT be published until real, documented proof exists.**

- Customer or client counts; named clients; client logos.
- Testimonials, quotes or endorsements.
- Case studies and any case-study metrics.
- Revenue, cost savings, ROI or other financial impact figures.
- Uptime/SLA figures achieved for AU4 clients (e.g. "99.9%").
- Number of products delivered (e.g. "40+"), projects completed, or success/win rates.
- Number of countries served or offices; years in business / company history / milestones.
- Team size / headcount or hiring-growth claims.
- Company-held certifications, awards, or publication history.
- Vendor **partnership** or "certified partner" status (experience ≠ partnership).
- Words like "proven", "trusted by", "award-winning", "world-class", "industry-leading" used in a way that implies evidence not yet held.
